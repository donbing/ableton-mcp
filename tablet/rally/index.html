How to play, and settings

The ball crosses the whole keyboard in one bar, landing on the beat. When it reaches an end, play any key in that end's octave while the ball is in it (the octave glows amber, brighter as the ball comes in; on the beat, as it reaches the octave, is best). Left hand at the bass end, right hand at the treble end. Each return is a little faster; a miss starts again from the end that missed. Any piano key starts a game. Without a piano: tap the end boxes, or press Z (bass), M (treble) or Space (whichever end the ball is heading for).
